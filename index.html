ℹ️ Statutory UK Repayment & Interest Rules for Plan 2:

• Repayment Threshold: You only pay 9% on income earned above £27,295/year (£2,274/month). If you earn less, your deduction is £0.

• Interest Calculation: Under Plan 2, interest while studying is RPI + 3% (7.1%). After graduation, it scales between RPI (4.1%) up to RPI + 3% (7.1%) depending on income. Under Plan 5, interest is strictly capped at RPI (4.1%).
